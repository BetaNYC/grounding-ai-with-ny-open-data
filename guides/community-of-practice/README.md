# Community of practice material

Decks and scripts for **peer communities of practice** working on civic AI: people who convene around AI literacy, safety, and governance rather than around one district, one classroom, or one newsroom.

## How this audience differs

Every other folder in this repo demos to someone who wants to see **their own** data. This audience is deciding **what to build and what to teach**, and some of the room starts from "AI is bad." So the deck leads with digital sovereignty as the frame, treats the tools as one piece of civic capacity, and ends with an ask to co-author curriculum rather than an ask to adopt a tool.
