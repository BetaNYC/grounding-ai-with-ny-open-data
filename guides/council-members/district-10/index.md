## What the session covers

- **Your district, right now.** What the city's own systems say is happening in Washington Heights
  and Inwood today.
- **Grounded against ungrounded.** The same question answered from the record and from an
  ordinary web search, side by side, so the difference is visible rather than asserted.
- **Your funding, from allocation to check.** Discretionary money as it appears in the Council's
  own disclosure, then as an actual disbursement in the Comptroller's system.
- **An issue you are working on**, sourced.

## What it is honest about

Every number carries the date it was checked (most recently 2026-10-19) in the presenter notes. **If a live call
disagrees with a figure in the script, trust the live call and say so out loud** — that is the demo
working, not failing.
