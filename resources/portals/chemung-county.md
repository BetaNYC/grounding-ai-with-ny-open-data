## Notes
- Free downloads (SHP, KMZ, some GDB) via DocumentCenter links: county boundary, road centerlines, surface water, flood zones, legislative districts, ZIP codes, census block groups, soils, DEM, aquifer, transit, trails.
- Orthoimages by request to the Planning Department.
- The directory's mention of a Schneider/Beacon parcel viewer was not re-checked on this pass.
- No Hub or Socrata catalog found (ArcGIS site search for 'Chemung' returned 0).

## How to query it
No public API. Files download directly, e.g. https://chemungcountyny.gov/DocumentCenter/View/16670/RoadCenterLine_0322 (not fetched in this pass).
