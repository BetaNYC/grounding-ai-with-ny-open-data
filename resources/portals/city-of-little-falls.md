## Notes

- Herkimer County (registry: no-portal) is the county source.
- Traps: cityoflittlefalls.com and 'Lead Safe Community - Little Falls' (org q9rik8Don3p98Q8m) are Little Falls, **Minnesota**; Socrata hits on data.montgomerycountymd.gov and an MDOT SHA Hub are **Maryland**. NYCOM's directory entry should be corrected.
