## Notes
- The police department publishes monthly PDF lists: arrests, report blotter and aggravated assaults, under "Monthly Data" (/1662), plus Civilian Complaint Data (/1751) and Calls for Service (/1958). They are documents, not datasets.
- The /1828 "Transparency" page requires a sign-in.
- The earlier note's "vendor-hosted GIS map viewer" was not found or re-verified this pass. The city relies on the Westchester County GeoHub for county-level layers.
