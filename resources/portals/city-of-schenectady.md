## Notes
- **Status settled** (was UNCONFIRMED): despite its name, the page is a list of PDF reports, last updated July 2021. It is not a data catalog.
- The earlier pass's "portal in Rochester's ArcGIS Hub namespace" lead was not re-traced. The Hub search found only "Housing ONE Schenectady Together (HOST)", a University of Denver-owned project site. Do not cite either as a city catalog.
- The Interactive Parking Regulation Map (/676) is a viewer.
- The city is commonly conflated with Schenectady County, which has no portal either.
