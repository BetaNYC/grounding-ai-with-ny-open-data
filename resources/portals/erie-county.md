## Notes
- 2025 tax parcel data is free but released by **request form**, not by download (press page dated 2026-01-28).
- Election district shapefiles: https://elections.erie.gov/ShapeDownload.
- The county's Resources page points to Open Data Buffalo, the NYS GIS Clearinghouse and data.gov.
- Traps: `ecgis-eriecopa.hub.arcgis.com` and related `eriecopa` Hubs are Erie County, **Pennsylvania**; `ecgisab.opendata.arcgis.com` centers on Erie County, **Ohio**. Excluded.

## How to query it
