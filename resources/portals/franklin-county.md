- The Development Authority of the North Country (DANC) publishes Franklin infrastructure and zoning layers on its public REST server: https://maps.dancgis.org/server/rest/services (Franklin_Infrastructure_P_24, Franklin_Special_Dis_Zoning_P_24).
- Trap: `data-franklincova.opendata.arcgis.com` is Franklin County, **Virginia**. Other 'Franklin County' Hubs are Ohio (`fca`), Pennsylvania and Iowa. All excluded.
