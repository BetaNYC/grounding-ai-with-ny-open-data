## Notes
- County GIS data flows through the NYS GIS Clearinghouse (https://data.gis.ny.gov/), not a county-run portal.
- The assessment database is at https://rensselaercounty.prosgar.com/.
- "Rensselaer Clean Mobility" (a LaBella-owned Hub) is a consultant project site, not a county catalog. The City of Rensselaer is a separate place.
